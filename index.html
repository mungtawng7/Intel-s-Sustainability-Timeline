1993
Energy Efficiency Achieved

Energy Star Pioneering

Intel partnered with the EPA to develop Energy Star standards for personal computers, launching low-power sleep mode CPU architectures.

  • Reduced idle power draw by over 60%.
  • Established initial eco-standards for computing silicon.
2020
Water Restoration Achieved

RISE 2020 Goals & Water Savings

Achieved 100% net positive water restoration across multiple manufacturing sites and restored billions of gallons of freshwater to local watersheds.

  • 7.1 Billion gallons of water conserved.
  • In-house water reclamation plants built globally.
2030
Renewable Power In Progress

100% Renewable Electricity

Targeting 100% renewable electricity usage across global operations and zero waste to landfills across semiconductor fabrication plants.

  • 93% achieved as of recent reporting.
  • Solar arrays & wind purchase agreements expanded.
2040
Net-Zero GHG Future Target

Net-Zero Greenhouse Gas Emissions

Committed to reaching net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2) across all global operations by 2040.

  • Investing $300M+ in energy efficiency projects.
  • Ultra-low carbon chip manufacturing technologies.
